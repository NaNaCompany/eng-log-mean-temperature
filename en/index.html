Enter values and select Calculate.

FORMULA

ΔT_lm = (ΔT₁−ΔT₂)/ln(ΔT₁/ΔT₂)

Assumptions

Required: ΔT₁,ΔT₂>0 without a temperature cross. Equal differences return their common limiting value. Heat duty, correction factors and exchanger geometry are excluded.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
